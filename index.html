Example signal Liquidity language changed

"Sufficient for the next twelve months" becomes "may seek additional financing", coupled with insider sells. Plainfilings highlights the changed language, and classifies the events, links the source text, and suggests the next checks: dilution risk, debt covenants, insider activity, and peer financing patterns.

Our model is trained on filings from multiple markets. Summaries pull out key risks, revenue figures, guidance and material events. Every point links back to the source text so you can check it in one click.

Plainfilings is NLP-first. Before any LLM is involved, our platform uses a purpose-built search system to extract key facts, detect changes, identify relevant language, and surface sentiment across disclosures. Its only after that, to summarize findings and explain complex language, that an LLM gets to do anything of relevance. In short; the core signal engine is not a chatbot. The LLM helps translate signals into something short and digestable.

We support all major filing types across our covered markets: annual reports, quarterly filings, material event disclosures, IPO prospectuses, proxy statements and more.
