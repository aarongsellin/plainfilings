The Problem

Reading stock filings
is hard and time consuming.

A filing can run 200+ pages of circular legal language written by lawyers. By the time you've decoded it, the market has already moved.

So most people wait for the analyst note. Which lands 15 minutes late, filtered through a sell-side relationship and a price target that needs defending. That’s not the filing. That’s someone’s spin on it.

The workings.

How it works.

01

Search a ticker

Enter any ticker symbol. plainfilings monitors regulatory feeds (SEC EDGAR, Nasdaq Stockholm, Copenhagen, Helsinki and Oslo Børs) and watches each company’s investor relations page directly, so we catch disclosures the moment they are published, wherever they appear first.

02

We read it for you

Our model reads the entire filing and picks out risks, key numbers and material changes. We run on our own hardware, so summaries are ready in 2–3 seconds from the moment a filing is published.

03

Read, act, move on

Get a structured plain-English summary in seconds. No legal dictionary required.

Integrations

Integrations for the tools
traders already use

Turn filings into real-time signals across your charts, alerts, chat rooms, broker workflows, spreadsheets, and custom systems.

Filing intelligence is most valuable when it appears where decisions happen. Connect real-time summaries, event tags, urgency scores, and filing links to the platforms traders already rely on — so every material filing can become an alert, a chart context, a team signal, or a programmable event.

TradingView

Bring filing events into your charting workflow. Link alerts directly to TradingView charts, align filings with price action, and spot when regulatory events coincide with breakouts, volume spikes, VWAP moves, or key technical levels.

Pine Script Workflows

Pair technical alerts with filing intelligence. Create trader-focused rules like “new 8-K plus price above VWAP,” “Form 4 insider buy plus breakout,” or “offering detected plus loss of support.”

Broker & Terminal Workflows

Connect filings to the places trades are monitored and executed. Match alerts against watchlists, positions, and options exposure, then hand off the ticker to the broker or terminal workflow where the trader acts.

Discord, Slack & Telegram

Route high-signal filing alerts into the channels where traders move fastest. Separate alerts by watchlist, sector, market cap, event type, or urgency — from insider buying and offerings to M&A, guidance changes, delistings, and bankruptcy events.

Webhooks & API

Make every filing programmable. Stream structured filing events into bots, scanners, dashboards, quant workflows, internal tools, and trading infrastructure.

Spreadsheets & Research Tools

Pull filing intelligence into Google Sheets, Excel, CSV exports, and research workflows for screening, monitoring, reporting, and idea generation.
