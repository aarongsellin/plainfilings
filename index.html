The Problem

Reading stock filings
is hard and time consuming.

A filing can run 200+ pages of circular legal language written by lawyers. By the time you've decoded it, the market has already moved. You deserve better than that.

The workings.

How it works.

01

Search a ticker

Enter any ticker symbol. plainfilings pulls every filing type directly from the exchange: US markets via SEC EDGAR, Nordic markets via Nasdaq Stockholm, Copenhagen, Helsinki and Oslo Børs.

02

We read it for you

Our model reads the entire filing and picks out risks, key numbers and material changes. We run on our own hardware, so summaries are ready in 2–3 seconds from the moment a filing is published.

03

Read, act, move on

Get a structured plain-English summary in seconds. No legal dictionary required.
