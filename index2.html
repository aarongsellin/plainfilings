Features

What you get.

Never miss a material disclosure

Key risks, revenue changes and material events are pulled out and listed clearly. Get notified by e-mail or SMS the moment a new filing drops.

Track the filings that matter

Unlimited watchlists let you monitor every ticker you follow. New filing drops? You’ll know before the crowd.

2–3 second summaries, always

We run our own models on our own hardware. No third-party API queues. A filing is published and your summary is ready in under three seconds.

Filing-to-filing comparison

See exactly what changed between this filing and the previous one: new risk factors, revised guidance, shifts in language around debt or margins.

Connect to your trading stack

Route filing summaries to TradingView, Discord, Slack, Telegram, broker workflows, spreadsheets, and custom APIs via REST, webhooks, and WebSocket streams.

The filing. Not someone’s take on it.

We pull from regulatory feeds and official exchange disclosure systems directly. No analyst relationship, no price target to defend. The summary reflects what the company disclosed.
